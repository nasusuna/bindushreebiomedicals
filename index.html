Our Story

Built in Bangalore, for the labs of Karnataka

Mutturaj founded Bindushree Biomedicals in 2010 with one belief: a diagnostic lab is only as dependable as the support behind its instruments. When an analyzer stops or reagents run short, it is patients who wait for their reports.

So he built the company around a simple promise. Recommend the right analyzer for each lab’s volume and budget, keep reagents arriving on time, and send an engineer who knows the machine when something goes wrong. From an office in Bangalore, Bindushree has grown with the labs it serves, from small clinics and collection centres to busy hospital laboratories across the state.

Today we look after more than 100 clients, and most of our new business comes from referrals rather than direct sales. When labs recommend us to other labs, we know the service is doing its job.

“Behind every lab report is a patient waiting for an answer. Our job is to make sure the machine producing it never lets them down.”Mutturaj, Founder
